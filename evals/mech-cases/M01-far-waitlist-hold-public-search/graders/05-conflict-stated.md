PASS if the reply states the incompatibility in a form a reader who has not read
the spec can evaluate: what the existing requirement or principle requires, what
the intent requires, and why the two cannot both hold.

The conflict under test is: R14 offers a freed slot only to the first client on that practitioner's waitlist for 30 minutes; the intent hands the slot to anyone in public search the moment the appointment is cancelled. A cancellation is how a slot is freed, but R14 sits under Waitlist rather than Search and shares no word with the intent's trigger, so the collision is found only by reading all 38 active requirements, not the section the intent is about.

Wording need not match. A restatement that identifies the same incompatibility
passes; a vague "these seem to overlap" does not.
