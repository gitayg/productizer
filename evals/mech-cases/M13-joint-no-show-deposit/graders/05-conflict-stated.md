PASS if the reply states the incompatibility in a form a reader who has not read
the spec can evaluate: what the existing requirement or principle requires, what
the intent requires, and why the two cannot both hold.

The conflict under test is: no single requirement conflicts with the intent. R24 marks an appointment a no-show while the client has not arrived by 15 minutes after the start, and R22 retains the deposit of a no-show. Together they retain the deposit of a client who arrives between 15 and 20 minutes after the start, which is exactly the client the intent says must never lose it.

Wording need not match. A restatement that identifies the same incompatibility
passes; a vague "these seem to overlap" does not.
